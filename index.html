Consumer Rights under COPRA 2019
Right #1

Right to Safety

Protection against goods and services hazardous to life and property. Requires compulsory quality certifications like ISI, FSSAI, or Agmark before sale in markets.

Right #2

Right to Information

Right to be informed about quality, quantity, potency, purity, standard, and price of goods. Governed under Legal Metrology Rules requiring MRP, ingredients, and expiry dates.

Right #3

Right to Choose

Assurance of access to a variety of goods and services at competitive prices without monopolistic coercion or forced tie-in sales by traders.

Right #4

Right to be Heard

Right to advocate consumer interests in appropriate forums, consumer grievance cells, and receive due consideration before authorities.

Right #5

Right to Seek Redressal

Right to seek legal remedy against unfair trade practices or exploitation, including replacement, full refund, defect removal, or financial compensation.

Right #6

Right to Consumer Education

Right to acquire knowledge and skill to be an informed consumer throughout life, supported by national awareness campaigns like Jago Grahak Jago.

Key Indian Consumer Acts & Frameworks
Primary Act

Consumer Protection Act (COPRA), 2019

Modernized legal protections introducing:

  • CCPA: Central authority to recall hazardous products and penalize misleading ads.
  • E-Commerce Rules: Mandates country-of-origin disclosure and quick grievance redressal.
  • Product Liability: Strict liability on manufacturers for defective goods.
Food Safety

Food Safety & Standards Act (FSSAI), 2006

Regulates the food sector:

  • Mandatory 14-digit FSSAI license numbers on all packaged foods.
  • Strict penalties against food adulteration and misleading health claims.
  • Clear Green (Veg) and Red (Non-Veg) dot indicators.
Quality Standards

Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) Act, 2016

Governs product standard certification:

  • ISI Mark: Mandatory for electrical equipment, gas cylinders, and helmets.
  • BIS Hallmark: 6-digit HUID code compulsory for gold jewelry purity.
  • AGMARK: Quality seal for agricultural commodities.
Weights & Measures

Legal Metrology Act, 2009

Regulates weights, measures, and package declarations:

  • Selling above Maximum Retail Price (MRP) is illegal and punishable.
  • Mandates Net Quantity, Expiry Date, and Customer Care details.
3-Tier Pecuniary Jurisdiction under COPRA 2019

District Commission

Jurisdiction: Handles claims where the value of goods and services paid does NOT exceed ₹1 Crore.

State Commission

Jurisdiction: Handles claims exceeding ₹1 Crore up to ₹10 Crore.

National Commission (NDRCD)

Jurisdiction: Handles major claims exceeding ₹10 Crore.
